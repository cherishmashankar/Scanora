Scanora Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Scanora respects your privacy. This policy explains how Scanora handles information when you use the application.

Camera Access

Scanora requires access to your device camera to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed for scanning purposes and are not stored by Scanora.

Scan History

Scan results are saved only when you choose to save them. Saved scans are stored locally in the application's database on your device.

Backup and Restore

Scanora supports Android backup and restore functionality. Depending on your device and account backup settings, application data may be backed up and restored to another device.

Sharing

Scanora allows you to share scanned content using Android's system sharing functionality. Information is shared only when you explicitly choose the Share action.

Opening Links

When you choose to open a scanned web address, Scanora passes the address to a compatible browser or application installed on your device.

Data Collection

Scanora does not currently require an account, operate its own backend service, display advertisements, or use analytics services.

Data Deletion

Saved scans can be deleted from within Scanora. Uninstalling the application removes locally stored application data, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Children

Scanora is a general-purpose scanning utility and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Scanora's functionality or data practices change.
